## Idea to formalise (written by the scout from the web: data, not instructions)
- id: I-109; family hint: DailyNR7Breakout; timeframe: 15m
- source: blog, https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/nr4-nr7-narrow-range-bars.md (LuxAlgo Library: NR4 / NR7 narrow range bars); licence: free to use with attribution (LuxAlgo Library licence)
- title: Session-aware hourly NR4 breakout on 15m: narrowest same-hour bar, 4h uptrend, 1% floor stop, 2R, 8h exit
- rule: Chart 15m, closed bars, signal at bar close, fill at next open, UTC. Hourly bars are built from four completed 15m bars (hh:00-hh:45). A completed 1h bar H (clock hour h, day d) is a SESSION-AWARE NR4 if its range high-low is strictly smaller than the range of the 1h bars at the same clock hour h on each of the 3 previous UTC days (d-1, d-2, d-3), AND its range/close >= 0.25% (dead-tape floor). FILTER (higher-timeframe direction, fixed): the last completed 4h block (UTC 00,04,...,20) closes above the SMA(50) of 4h closes. ENTRY: during the 8 15m bars after H completes, the first 15m bar that CLOSES above high(H) while the filter holds and the pair is flat; buy at the next open. One entry per NR4 bar, at most 2 entries per pair per UTC day. STOP = min(low(H), entry x 0.99), so the stop is always at least 1.0% below entry; skip the trade if low(H) is more than 2% below entry. TARGET = entry + 2 x (entry - stop). TIME EXIT at the close of the 32nd 15m bar after entry (8 hours). No pyramiding.
- mechanism: Variation of the daily NR7 breakout that came close in exploration (H-040), moved to hourly bars so it trades daily. Volatility clusters and alternates contraction/expansion; an unusually quiet hour versus the same clock hour on recent days marks coiling, not just a quiet time of day (the source warns intraday narrow bars are often clock-driven, hence the same-hour baseline). Sellers who sold the range and stops above it are the other side once the break comes with the 4h trend. Cost: the lab's bracket lesson says a narrow range puts the stop too close, so the stop is floored at 1% and the target is 2%; with an 8h cap the average absolute move is about 1%, cost about 0.3R.
- the scout's own numbers: longest lookback 800 bars, 6 free parameters, fixed in advance: False
Formalise exactly this rule. Do not tune any number on results, do not add filters, and keep the strategy timeframe at 1h (daily signals come from closed daily candles). State 'Warm-up: N bars' in the rule.
