## Scope
- Pairs: BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT, BNB/USDT. Timeframe: 1h. Spot, long only, one open trade at a time, whole balance per trade.

## Cost scenarios (the backtest charges these on every entry and every exit)
| scenario | cost per side | round trip (entry + exit) |
|---|---|---|
| optimistic | 0.100% | 0.200% |
| base | 0.175% | 0.350% |
| conservative | 0.250% | 0.500% |
| stress | 0.475% | 0.950% |

Net profit must be positive at the base AND conservative cost, and it must exceed a bar that rises with every earlier variant in the family. A strategy that is profitable only at the optimistic cost has no edge after costs.

## Direction from the owner (steers the search; does not change any gate or limit)
Focus this search on drawdown control and robustness, not on maximizing historical return.

Why: H-002 lost money (about 800 trades per pair, cost-dominated). H-005 returned +500% to +880% but its maximum drawdown was 43% to 51%, against the 25% gate. Its worst drawdown on each pair was a streak of 8 to 13 consecutive losing trades (average loss 6% to 8% each, whole balance per trade) in a bear or range market after the 2021 peak, with at most one winner inside it. Do not repeat that pattern.

Guidelines:
1. Look for risk-control mechanisms: trend or regime filters, entry confirmation, volatility filters, exit conditions. Each must have a stated reason tied to the failure above; no unjustified indicator combinations, no parameter tuning.
2. Aim for max drawdown below 25% in all four cost scenarios with positive expected return. Remember that with a whole balance per trade, N consecutive losses of L% each must stay under the 25% ceiling.
3. Keep trade count in mind: fewer than 30 trades in a split is "inconclusive", and a filter that is too restrictive will land there (H-004 had 13 to 16 trades). Equally, very frequent signals are eaten by costs (H-002).
4. The same rule and parameters must work on BTC, ETH and BNB. No per-pair tuning.
5. Spot, long only, 1h. Sizing methods such as custom_stake_amount are forbidden by the strategy check, so risk must be controlled by entries and exits.
6. Respect the variant cap of 3 per family. Prefer a new family over spending a variant of a family that already failed, unless you can name the specific mechanism that the variant fixes.
7. In the proposal rationale, state the technical justification, the expected mechanism of risk reduction, and objective success criteria (the existing gates), before anything is implemented.

The gates, limits and protocol are unchanged. Do not design the rule to make approval easier; design it to be a strategy that could survive the validation split, which you cannot see.

### H-004 - SlowTimeSeriesMomentum (invalid: implementation deviated from rule)
Rule: Use closed 1h candles only; signals evaluated at bar close, order executed at next bar open. Long only, one position, whole balance. ENTRY: close > highest high of the prior 1440 bars (60 days, excluding current bar) AND close > SMA(4800 bars, 200 days) of close AND no open position. EXIT: close < lowest low of the prior 720 bars (30 days, excluding current bar), OR close below entry price by 15% (hard stop, checked on closed bars). After any exit, no re-entry until at least 168 bars (7 days) have passed. No other filters, no pyramiding, no take-profit.

### H-005 - VolatilitySqueezeBreakout (rejected at exploration)
Rule: Closed 1h candles only; signals evaluated at bar close, order executed at next bar open. Long only, one position, whole balance. Definitions (all use completed bars): R(t) = (highest high of the 168 bars ending at t - lowest low of the 168 bars ending at t) / close(t). Squeeze(t) is true if R(t) is <= the 20th percentile of R over the 2160 bars ending at t-1 (rolling quantile 0.20, excluding the current bar). ENTRY at close of bar t when ALL hold: (1) Squeeze was true on at least one of the previous 24 bars (t-24..t-1); (2) close(t) > highest high of the prior 168 bars (t-168..t-1, excluding current bar); (3) close(t) > SMA(2400) of close; (4) no open position; (5) cooldown satisfied. EXIT at close of bar t when close(t) < lowest low of the prior 336 bars (t-336..t-1, excluding current), or via the hard stop. STOP-LOSS: fixed 8% below entry price (native intrabar stop).
