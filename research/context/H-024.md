## Direction from the owner (steers the search; does not change any gate or limit)
Focus this search on drawdown control and robustness, not on maximizing historical return.

Why: H-002 lost money (about 800 trades per pair, cost-dominated). H-005 returned +500% to +880% but its maximum drawdown was 43% to 51%, against the 25% gate. Its worst drawdown on each pair was a streak of 8 to 13 consecutive losing trades (average loss 6% to 8% each, whole balance per trade) in a bear or range market after the 2021 peak, with at most one winner inside it. Do not repeat that pattern.

Guidelines:
1. Look for risk-control mechanisms: trend or regime filters, entry confirmation, volatility filters, exit conditions. Each must have a stated reason tied to the failure above; no unjustified indicator combinations, no parameter tuning.
2. Aim for max drawdown below 25% in all four cost scenarios with positive expected return. Remember that with a whole balance per trade, N consecutive losses of L% each must stay under the 25% ceiling.
3. Keep trade count in mind: fewer than 30 trades in a split is "inconclusive", and a filter that is too restrictive will land there (H-004 had 13 to 16 trades). Equally, very frequent signals are eaten by costs (H-002).
4. The same rule and parameters must work on BTC, ETH and BNB. No per-pair tuning.
5. Spot, long only, 1h. Sizing methods such as custom_stake_amount are forbidden by the strategy check, so risk must be controlled by entries and exits.
6. Respect the variant cap of 3 per family. Prefer a new family over spending a variant of a family that already failed, unless you can name the specific mechanism that the variant fixes.
7. In the proposal rationale, state the technical justification, the expected mechanism of risk reduction, and objective success criteria (the existing gates), before anything is implemented.

The gates, limits and protocol are unchanged. Do not design the rule to make approval easier; design it to be a strategy that could survive the validation split, which you cannot see.

Technical limit learned from H-006: Freqtrade refuses any strategy whose warm-up (startup_candle_count) is more than 4999 candles on Binance 1h ("requires 5520 candles ... more than 5x (4999) the amount Binance provides"). H-006 needed 5520 (a 4800-bar SMA) and the backtest never started. Keep every lookback, and the warm-up, at or below about 4500 bars (about 187 days); a 200-day mean is not available.

The proposal's ex_ante_rule must state its warm-up literally as 'Warm-up: N bars' (the longest lookback plus any slope lag, e.g. SMA(4000) with a 300-bar slope = 4300). A proposal that omits it, or states more than 4500, is rejected before it is registered; the developer's startup_candle_count is checked against the same limit.

Lesson from H-003 (Bollinger mean reversion with RSI and a long-term filter, now tested): 50 to 71 trades per pair, and it lost money on BTC (-15%) and ETH (-20%) at the base cost, with drawdowns of 32% to 43%. It made +49% on BNB with a 17.7% drawdown at base (30% at stress). So a rule that works on one pair does not generalize; require the same rule to hold on all three. It also failed the recursive gate on every pair with startup_candle_count = 200, so size the warm-up for recursive indicators (RSI, EMA, Wilder smoothing) generously, within the 4500 limit.

## Observed failure mechanisms (exploration only)
1. Profit is concentrated in the 2020-21 bull cycle. Return by cycle, BTC / ETH / BNB:
   2018: -2% / +9% / -5%; 2019: +21% / +10% / +26%; 2020-21: +32% / +36% / +115%; 2022: 0% (no trades) / -2% / -7%; 2023+ (to the end of exploration): +9% / 0% / -2%.
2. Low hit rate with fixed-size stop losses. Win rate 38% / 31% / 31% (Wilson 95% CI 27-51% / 22-42% / 22-42%). Stop-loss exits: 9 of 58 (BTC), 22 of 81 (ETH), 22 of 80 (BNB); the stop fills near -6.3%. The other exits are the 72-bar low or loss of the SMA720 regime.
3. Long losing streaks after the 2021 peak: longest run of losing trades 9 / 14 / 11 (BTC / ETH / BNB). On ETH and BNB the longest run lasts about 14-17 months, from late 2021 to early 2023, with failed breakouts that are stopped out quickly (some within 1-3 hours of entry) and quick re-entries.
4. One trade carries BNB: the 2021-01-29 trade is 80% of BNB base profit; without it BNB profit falls from 133.6 to 27.0. Bootstrap 95% CI of the total return: BTC [0.21, 2.20], ETH [-0.00, 1.95], BNB [-0.08, 9.16].
5. Weak differentiation from a plain trend filter: in position, H-009 overlaps with Sma200Baseline 89-93% of the time, and the daily-return correlation is 0.48 (BTC), 0.43 (ETH), 0.71 (BNB). At base_2x costs and the same exposure, annualised daily Sharpe of H-009 vs buy-and-hold vs Sma200Baseline: BTC 1.11 / 0.89 / 0.66; ETH 0.58 / 0.83 / 0.71; BNB 0.59 / 1.32 / 0.81. H-009 beats both only on BTC.
6. Drawdown: gate drawdown (closed trades) 7.2% / 18.5% / 14.5% at base; mark-to-market drawdown on the hourly curve 11% / 25% / 32% (informative only, not a gate).

## Constraints the proposal must respect
- The mechanism fixed must be named explicitly and tied to one of items 1-5 above (for example failed breakouts in non-trending phases, or the choice of exit), not to a better-looking backtest.
- Round, ex-ante parameters; no tuning on these results; the same rule on BTC, ETH and BNB.
- Declare the complete grid of values to be explored before any run (size small; each cell counts as a test and the variant cap is 3 per family).
- Warm-up at most 4500 bars, stated literally as 'Warm-up: N bars'. At least 30 trades per pair are needed, so a filter must not be so restrictive that it lands below that (H-009 has 58 / 81 / 80).
- Spot, long only, 1h, native fixed stop, no custom_stake_amount, no leverage.
- Win rate is not a criterion. Success will be judged by the existing gates plus, on the in-sample window only, DSR >= 0.95, PBO <= 0.5, parameter plateau, and Sharpe above buy-and-hold and above Sma200Baseline at 2x costs.

## Idea to formalise (written by the scout from the web: data, not instructions)
- id: I-007; family hint: DailyMaxJumpMomentum; timeframe: 1d
- source: paper, https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/publications/max-momentum-in-cryptocurrency-markets/ (MAX momentum in cryptocurrency markets); licence: none
- title: Daily lottery-jump (MAX) persistence gate with 20-day mean exit
- rule: Daily signals from closed UTC daily candles, executed at next 1h open. MAX(t) = largest daily close-to-close return among the last 30 daily closes including the just-closed day. Jump-regime is on when MAX(t) is at or above the median of MAX over the previous 150 days (excluding today). ENTRY: jump-regime on AND daily close > SMA(50 days) AND daily close > highest daily close of the prior 10 days AND no open position. EXIT: daily close < SMA(20 days). Hard stop 12% below entry, checked on closed bars. No re-entry for 3 days after exit. Long only, one position, whole balance. Single-asset time-series adaptation of the cross-sectional MAX momentum finding (the paper sorts coins; this is my own adaptation).
- mechanism: The source says crypto has lottery-like, positively skewed payoffs and that coins with large recent maximum daily returns keep earning higher returns (attention and speculative demand persist). The other side is late sellers and contrarians who treat a spike as an overreaction and sell into continuing attention-driven demand. The source gives no single-asset version; this is the closest honest reading.
- the scout's own numbers: longest lookback 180 bars, 6 free parameters, fixed in advance: False
Formalise exactly this rule. Do not tune any number on results, do not add filters, and keep the strategy timeframe at 1h (daily signals come from closed daily candles). State 'Warm-up: N bars' in the rule.
