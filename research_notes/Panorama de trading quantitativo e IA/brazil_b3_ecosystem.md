# Brazilian B3 futures quant ecosystem (WIN / WDO), 2025–2026

Research date: 2026-11-21. About 20 web searches and fetches. Many primary B3 pages are PDFs or are rendered with JavaScript, so a few key numbers come from search snippets or B3-hosted educational pages instead of the full tariff manual. Each such case is flagged below.

## 1. Historical data sources for WIN/WDO

### Takeaway
B3 itself no longer offers a free trade-by-trade download for derivatives. The old FTP (ftp.bmf.com.br) was shut down in 2020, and its files moved to the paid UP2DATA service. Free B3 bulletins are only daily summaries. For history with every trade and the aggressor side, the realistic choices are licensed distributors (Nelogica/Profit, ICE and others) or recording the data yourself from a live feed (MT5 ticks, Profit, UMDF).

### Cited Findings
- B3 deactivated the legacy BM&FBOVESPA/Cetip portals and the FTP server ftp.bmf.com.br on 29 June 2020. Earlier circulars had set 30/11/2018 and 14/11/2019 as shutdown dates. — [B3 unification notice](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/unificacao.htm); [B3 portals completion](https://clientes.b3.com.br/en/w/b3-conclui-unificacao-de-seus-sites); [CE 011/2018-VPC Revisão de Arquivos](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/55/B3/80/0E/92CD4610DB939D46AC094EA8/CE%20011-2018-VPC%20-%20Revisao%20de%20Arquivos.pdf)
- The CE 011/2018 circular says the FTP data files "serão disponibilizados pelo serviço UP2DATA". UP2DATA delivers trade-information files in TXT, CSV, XML and/or JSON. — [CE 011/2018](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/55/B3/80/0E/92CD4610DB939D46AC094EA8/CE%20011-2018-VPC%20-%20Revisao%20de%20Arquivos.pdf); [UP2DATA available data](https://www.b3.com.br/en_us/market-data-and-indices/data-services/up2data/available-data/); [UP2DATA FAQ](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/market-data-e-indices/servicos-de-dados/up2data/perguntas-frequentes/)
- The free "pesquisa por pregão" daily bulletins (for example BD_Final) give a summary of all trades per contract, not individual trades. — [B3 descrição dos arquivos](https://b3.com.br/pt_br/market-data-e-indices/servicos-de-dados/market-data/historico/boletins-diarios/pesquisa-por-pregao/descricao-dos-arquivos/); [B3 pesquisa por pregão](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/market-data-e-indices/servicos-de-dados/market-data/historico/boletins-diarios/pesquisa-por-pregao/)
- B3 says that for historical products not on its site it works through authorized distributors. It publishes a Market Data Consumption Policy and a Market Data Commercial Policy (v3.0.4.1) that govern licensing, including redistribution and non-display use. — [UP2DATA FAQ](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/market-data-e-indices/servicos-de-dados/up2data/perguntas-frequentes/); [Política de Consumo de Market Data V1.2.1](https://b3.com.br/data/files/4F/25/23/55/5D881A10F75B881AAC094EA8/Politica%20de%20Consumo%20de%20Market%20Data%20B3%20V1.2.1.pdf); [Política Comercial de Market Data v3.0.4.1](https://b3.com.br/data/files/6D/77/9D/65/0AB929106EEC8429AC094EA8/Politica%20Comercial%20de%20Market%20Data%20v3.0.4.1.pdf)
- B3 DataWise+ consolidates listed-market information through dashboards, files and APIs. Its tick-level derivatives coverage was not verified. — [DataWise available data](https://www.b3.com.br/en_us/market-data-and-indices/data-services/datawise/available-data/)
- ICE Data Services sells B3 (ex-BM&F) tick-by-tick Level 1/2/3 history, up to 15 years. — [ICE developer catalog: B3](https://developer.ice.com/fixed-income-data-services/catalog/b3-formerly-brazilian-mercantile-and-futures-exchange-bmf)
- The live feed is UMDF. Binary UMDF (SBE) message specs v2.3.1.x are dated June and August 2026, and their sections cover Trade, TradeCondition, TrdSubType and Trade Bust. The "UMDF PUMA Conflated" spec includes a trade-history request/response (UTHQ/UTHP). Getting the feed directly needs B3 connectivity and certification (the UMDF 2.0 certification roadmap), so it is not a retail path. — [Binary UMDF Message Spec Guidelines v2.3.1.2](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/84/B7/1D/9E/AB611A103BBB511AAC094EA8/BinaryUMDF-MessageSpecificationGuidelines-v.2.3.1.2-enUS.pdf); [UMDF Conflated spec](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/39/F5/97/3F/17B719104FE62719AC094EA8/UMDFConflated_MarketDataSpecification.pdf); [Roteiro de certificação UMDF 2.0](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/68/21/9F/CA/17CAD610503056D6AC094EA8/Roteiro_de_certificacao_UMDF2.0_Portugues.pdf); [Política Comercial Conectividade](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/C9/92/5D/A6/EED1F910ADC36BE9AC094EA8/Politica%20Comercial%20Servicos%20de%20Conectividade.pdf)
- B3's public quotes page has a "Tick by tick" view under Derivatives. It appears to be for display only, not a bulk download. — [B3 Quotes](https://www.b3.com.br/en_us/market-data-and-indices/data-services/market-data/quotes/quotes/)
- On MT5, a user on the MQL5 forum says BTG does not offer the perpetual (continuous) series for WIN/WDO. This is a single user report, unverified. — [MQL5 forum, stock exchange](https://www.mql5.com/pt/forum/stock-exchange)

### Inferences
- The lab's current setup (5 years of 1m/5m bars from the Clear demo plus its own tick recording) is the normal retail path. A Nelogica quote for history with the aggressor flag is the most direct way to get order-flow data. ICE and UP2DATA are institutional-grade options and probably priced for institutions (not verified).
- Before relying on a demo server's history, check it against a second source (for example Profit's export, or the free daily BD_Final close, volume and number of trades per contract). Demo-server bars can differ from the exchange tape.
- MT5 tick history from brokers usually carries buy/sell flags (TICK_FLAG_BUY/SELL) taken from the B3 feed. How reliable those flags are on the Clear demo server, compared with Nelogica's aggressor field, should be measured on overlapping days. That is a check to run, not a sourced fact.

### Gaps
- No pricing found for Nelogica historical tick and aggressor data, ProfitDLL licensing, Cedro, or Economatica intraday futures data. Searches returned nothing primary; these vendors quote privately.
- No check on whether UMDF Trade messages carry an explicit aggressor-side field (the extracted spec text did not show it). The B3 PUMA feed is generally understood to carry it, but this is unconfirmed here.
- No sourced information on the BTG or XP API for market data.

## 2. Execution platforms, APIs, latency and costs

### Takeaway
Retail automation on B3 futures runs mainly through (a) MetaTrader 5 at Clear, XP, Rico, Genial, Órama, Terra, BTG and others, (b) Nelogica Profit with NTSL or ProfitDLL, and (c) hosted robot platforms such as SmarttBot. Brokerage fees on minicontracts are commonly zero. The real cost is B3 emolumentos plus registro, around R$0.25 per WIN contract per side for small day traders. Profits are taxed at 20% (day trade), with 1% IRRF withheld and a monthly DARF under code 6015.

Clear is described as popular with beginners for zero brokerage on stocks and futures, despite complaints about instability. — [Rankia: melhores corretoras para futuros](https://rankia.com.br/brokers/brokers-internacionais/melhores-corretoras-para-futuros-a-partir-do-brasil.html)
- Genial advertises MetaTrader 5 with "corretagem ZERO" on the main assets and a "corretagem zero em minicontrato" activation for WIN/WDO. — [Genial MetaTrader](https://www.genialinvestimentos.com.br/trader/plataformas/meta-trader/); [Genial: corretagem zero em minicontrato](https://www.genialinvestimentos.com.br/artigos/corretagem-zero-em-minicontrato/)
- XP publishes a platform comparison covering its trading platforms. — [XP comparativo de plataformas](https://web.xpi.com.br/xp/documentos/comparativo-de-plataformas)
- MetaQuotes keeps a broker finder for MT5. — [metatrader5.com find broker](https://www.metatrader5.com/pt/find-broker.md)
- Nelogica's native automation language is NTSL (Nelogica Trading System Language) inside Profit Pro. — [DIO article](https://www.dio.me/articles/programacao-e-financas-como-a-logica-do-trading-na-b3-acelera-sua-carreira-tech-em-ads-d4b9550b1cd8)
- Community writers describe automation through the Nelogica library (ProfitDLL) as order automation "mas não é HFT", with retail-level latency much higher than HFT. — [TabNews: as 67 variáveis do mini dólar](https://www.tabnews.com.br/macnator/as-67-variaveis-do-mini-dolar-por-que-seu-robo-vai-falhar-e-o-freela-que-prova-isso)
- A UFMG academic work on mini-index automation says complex strategies are easier in Python than in MQL5. — [UFMG repository](https://repositorio.ufmg.br/bitstreams/59bdf474-78a6-4e7b-a080-2b1d0608e47b/download)
- SmarttBot's own materials say robot configuration and activation are the client's responsibility and that it suits investors with an aggressive risk profile. Its ranking guide recommends judging robots over 1 year or since inception, by profit factor and drawdown, not over 1 to 3 months. — [SmarttBot ranking guide](https://intercom.help/smarttbot/pt-BR/articles/12945900-como-funciona-o-ranking-da-smarttbot-e-como-escolher-os-melhores-robos); [SmarttBot Ebook Cloud](https://smarttbot.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Ebook-Cloud.pdf)

### Cited Findings — B3 fees (emolumentos and registro)
- B3's Ibovespa futures fee page lists a settlement fee of BRL 0.30 for WIN and BRL 1.52 for IND, a contract factor and ADV weight of 0.2 for WIN (1 for IND), and a "price table by volume" whose single fee runs from BRL 1.97 (ADV 1–50) down to BRL 1.07 (ADV above 15,000). The day-trade reduction by day-trade ADV is 35% (1–5 contracts), 40% (6–50), 55% (51–150), 70% (151–1,500) and 75% (above 1,500). - When brokerage is charged, ISS/PIS/COFINS of about 9.65% applies to the brokerage fee. — [Genial: custo real do day trade](https://www.genialinvestimentos.com.br/artigos/custo-real-day-trade/) (search snippet)

### Cited Findings — taxes
- Day trade is taxed at 20% of monthly net profit. Brokers withhold 1% IRRF on day-trade gains ("dedo-duro"), which is credited against the tax due. The DARF uses code 6015 and is due on the last business day of the following month. There is no R$20k exemption for day trade. Day-trade losses offset only day-trade gains and can be carried forward if declared. Worked example: R$2,000 profit minus R$500 prior loss gives a base of R$1,500, tax of R$300, less R$20 IRRF, so a DARF of R$280. — [XP: day trade no imposto de renda](https://conteudos.xpi.com.br/guia-de-investimentos/relatorios/day-trade-no-imposto-de-renda/); [InfoMoney IR 2025](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=2827439); [Contábeis](https://www.contabeis.com.br/artigos/6931/tributacao-operacao-de-day-trade-e-swing-trade-bolsa-de-valores-na-pessoa-fisica/)
- MP 1303/2025 (11/06/2025) proposed a flat 17.5% rate for financial investments, including day trade. The Câmara took it off the agenda on 08/10/2025 (251 votes in favour, with 195 or 193 against depending on the source) and it lapsed. The prior rules returned: day trade at 20% under art. 2º §2º of Lei 11.033/2004. One source expects the topic to return in 2026 as a bill (unconfirmed). — [Planalto MP 1303](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2025/Mpv/mpv1303.htm); [Contábeis: a queda da MP 1303](https://www.contabeis.com.br/artigos/73399/a-queda-da-mp-1303-2025-o-que-muda-na-tributacao-de-investimentos-e-criptoativos/); [Câmara technical note](https://www2.camara.leg.br/orcamento-da-uniao/estudos/2025/mp-1303-2025-nota-tecnica-no-18-2025-cd-volpe)

### Inferences
- **The cost check is consistent.** The WIN contract factor of 0.2 times the lowest-tier fee of R$1.97, with the 35% small day-trade reduction, gives 0.2 × 1.97 × 0.65 ≈ **R$0.256 per contract per side**. That matches the lab's measured R$0.25/side and sits under B3's "max R$0.50". This is my own arithmetic on the B3 tables, not a figure B3 states. Moving to 6–50 day-trade contracts per day (40% reduction) gives about R$0.236.
- **Cost in ticks.** On WIN, a tick is R$1, so R$0.25 fee plus 0.4 tick slippage is about R$0.65 per side, or about 1.3 ticks (6.5 index points) per round trip. On WDO, a tick is R$5. Using the B3 maximum of R$2.12 and 0.4 tick slippage (R$2) gives about R$4.1 per side, or about 1.65 ticks per round trip. In ticks, WDO is the more expensive market for a small trader. That fits the lab's internal finding that "WDO is unfair at R$30/30" (that note is from internal memory, not this research).
- **Tax.** The 20% tax on net monthly profit with loss carry-forward does not change the sign of a gross edge, but it does cut realized Sharpe by about 20% of gains. A lab that expects about 500 trades a year per market needs monthly P&L tracking per CPF for the DARF.
- **Latency.** Retail MT5 and Profit routing go through the broker's order-management system. Latency is likely in the tens of milliseconds or more, compared with microseconds for co-located HFT (no sourced figure). Strategies that live on 0.4–1 tick edges therefore compete against HFT, which supports moving to rarer trades with larger targets.

### Gaps
- No primary 2025–2026 table found for **WDO** emolumentos per contract by ADV tier (only the R$2.12 maximum and the USD/PTAX mechanism). No separate **registro** fee figure was extracted for WIN or WDO.
- No published retail latency measurements for Clear/XP MT5 or Profit routing were found.
- No source found for DMA/FIX access for small traders (DMA 1–4 models, sponsored access minimums) or for Sinacor. Brokers do not publish this. Tryd details were not researched.
- No BTG/XP retail order API documentation was found.

## 3. Regulation (CVM)

### Takeaway
Running a robot on your own account is legal. Manipulation such as spoofing and layering is sanctioned whether done by a human or a robot. Offering signals, robots with fixed parameters, or copy-trade to others pulls the provider into CVM-regulated activity (Resolução CVM 20 analyst, or consultant or manager registration). Tools where the user sets all parameters have been treated as outside regulation in the literature.

### Cited Findings
- Resolução CVM 20 (25/02/2021) regulates securities analysts. One source says a company or platform offering copy-trade must have the trader accredited beforehand as an analyst (via APIMEC). — [Resolução CVM 20 consolidated](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/001/resol020consolid.pdf); [Monitor Mercantil](https://monitormercantil.com.br/resolucoes-sobre-intermediacao-de-operacoes-com-valores-mobiliarios/)
- An academic article on Brazilian order robots ("robôs de ordens") says that when the investor has full autonomy to set the parameters, CVM does not treat the activity as regulated and waives analyst accreditation. It classifies robots as closed-box, open-box and grey-box. The three providers it studied were APIMEC-accredited analysts. — [Revista Perspectivas Contemporâneas](https://revista2.grupointegrado.br/revista/index.php/perspectivascontemporaneas/article/download/3624/1376/18320)
- Using robots on the exchange is not illegal, but CVM has sanctioned spoofing carried out with robots. — [Capital Aberto: desafios regulatórios dos robôs](https://capitalaberto.com.br/artigos/robos-de-investimento-podem-manipular-o-mercado/); [Folha Vitória: CVM condena e multa manipulação](https://www.folhavitoria.com.br/economia/cvm-condena-e-multa-nova-manipulacao/); [CVM PAS vote, 2024](https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anexos/2024/20240305_pas_cvm_19957_007254_2021_11_diretor_daniel_maeda_voto.pdf)
- In 2022 CVM modernized its rules on organized markets and best execution of client orders. — [CVM news, 2022](https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2022/cvm-moderniza-regras-sobre-mercados-organizados-e-melhor-execucao-de-ordens-de-clientes)

### Inferences
- For a small lab trading one owner's CPF account, no CVM registration is needed. The legal risks are (a) market-abuse patterns such as rapid order placement and cancellation that look like layering, and (b) any later move to share signals or robots with others. A team of several people trading one person's account is a contractual and tax question rather than a CVM one. Pooling outside money would need Resolução 21 (manager) status or a fund structure. This follows the general structure of Resoluções 19, 20 and 21; I did not verify the text clause by clause.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch the texts of Resoluções CVM 19 (consultant) and 21 (manager). The exact boundaries for "sharing a strategy with a team" or selling an open-parameter robot need legal review.
- No B3/BSM rules on order-to-trade ratios or message limits for retail robots were found (B3 does have message-throttling and anti-layering surveillance; I did not find a source).

## 4. Brazilian quant community and published research

### Takeaway
The main empirical anchor is Chague, De-Losso & Giovannetti. Of 19,646 people who started day-trading WIN between 2013 and 2015, almost none earned even a minimum wage, and there was no evidence of learning. Open-source Brazilian code for WIN/WDO is thin. Most visible "robots" are commercial MQL5 or Hotmart products with unverified backtests. Academic ML work at USP, PUC-Rio and UFMG is mostly about stocks, crypto or high-frequency equity returns, not WIN directly.

### Cited Findings
- Chague, De-Losso & Giovannetti, "Day Trading for a Living?" (SSRN 3423101; FGV EESP TD 525). Using regulator (CVM) data on all 19,646 individuals who began day-trading mini-Ibovespa futures in 2013–2015 and persisted, it found it "virtually impossible" to day trade for a living. Only 1.1% earned more than the minimum wage and 0.5% more than a bank teller's starting salary. There was no evidence of learning. — [IDEAS/RePEc FGV EESP TD 525](https://ideas.repec.org/p/fgv/eesptd/525.html); [SSRN 3423101 (Scribd copy)](https://www.scribd.com/document/428540384/SSRN-id3423101); [Quantpedia summary](https://quantpedia.com/retail-day-trading-is-an-uphill-battle/)
- CXO Advisory's summary says about 3.0% had positive net daily returns. — [CXO Advisory](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/investing-expertise/day-trading-a-bust/) (secondary)
- FGV EESP published a follow-up on day trading during the COVID-19 pandemic. — [FGV EESP: pesquisa day trade e pandemia](https://portal.fgv.br/sites/default/files/uploads/eesp-pesquisa-day-trade-e-pandemia.pdf)
- Python tooling: MegaJoctan/StrategyTester5 emulates the MetaTrader5 Python API over terminal history so strategies can be backtested (`pip install strategytester5`). Its free version is less precise than the paid edition. — [GitHub StrategyTester5](https://github.com/MegaJoctan/StrategyTester5)
- Commercial WIN robots on the MQL5 Market (for example Abydos B3 MT5, with a WIN$ backtest from April 2020 to January 2021, and Titan Index EA) are vendor claims, not independently verified. — [Abydos B3 MT5](https://www.mql5.com/pt/market/product/61273); [Titan Index EA](https://www.mql5.com/pt/market/product/182604)
- A UFMG undergraduate thesis backtested robots on WIN and picked the 30 best that were profitable over two backtested years. That is in-sample selection, not live results. — [UFMG TCC, Pedro Araújo](https://repositorio.ufmg.br/bitstream/1843/38290/1/Pedro%20Ara%c3%bajo%20-%20TCC%20%2b%20Ata%20%2b%20Ficha%20Catalogr%c3%a1fica%20.pdf)
- Academic theses on intraday ML (none on WIN specifically): USP FEA 2023 (random forest on intraday Bitcoin direction), USP Poli 2023 (tree models for buy and sell timing), USP EACH 2021 (SVM plus genetic algorithm on intraday Brazilian stocks), PUC-Rio 2024 (high-frequency return forecasting, econometrics vs ML) and PUC-Rio 2022 (LSTM and random forest for intraday predictability with VIX). — [USP Poli 2023 FEA](https://www.teses.usp.br/teses/disponiveis/12/12139/tde-04092023-191410/publico/PaulodeCastroRubioPoliCorrigida.pdf); [USP Poli Santos 2023](https://www.teses.usp.br/teses/disponiveis/3/3139/tde-03012024-143028/publico/ThiagoRayamSouzaSantosCorr23.pdf); [USP EACH Frias 2021](https://www.teses.usp.br/teses/disponiveis/100/100132/tde-18052021-222755/publico/Dissertacao_corrigida_FredericoAlexandreSousaFrias.pdf); [PUC-Rio 69353](https://www.maxwell.vrac.puc-rio.br/69353/69353.PDF); [PUC-Rio 60253](https://www.maxwell.vrac.puc-rio.br/60253/60253.PDF); [Insper repository](https://repositorio-api.insper.edu.br/server/api/core/bitstreams/9299ed62-1c29-446e-8a69-1cf52e9d5047/content)
- A TabNews community essay ("as 67 variáveis do mini dólar") argues that most WDO retail robots fail because of microstructure and regime variables. Its stance is that ProfitDLL-based bots are retail automation and cannot compete with HFT. — [TabNews](https://www.tabnews.com.br/macnator/as-67-variaveis-do-mini-dolar-por-que-seu-robo-vai-falhar-e-o-freela-que-prova-isso)
- No independent study of SmarttBot user results was found. Marketing claims (for example "in over 99% of cases automation beats the individual", from sponsored content by another vendor) have no stated method. — [InfoMoney sponsored](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=3040698)

### Inferences
- The base rate is very negative. For individuals on WIN, profitable day trading after costs is rare, and the lab's own result (561 WIN variants, none passing) fits that prior. Published Brazilian research gives no documented, robust intraday WIN edge at retail cost levels. Edges are more likely in (a) order flow and aggressor imbalance, which needs the tick data being bought, (b) the opening and auction dynamics, and (c) lower-frequency or overnight effects. These are hypotheses, not sourced findings.

### Gaps

## 5. WIN/WDO microstructure facts

### Takeaway
WIN has a tick of 5 points = R$1 (0.2 × IBOV) and trades 09:00–18:25 under the 2026 schedule, with synchronized IND/WIN pre-opening auctions and a quantity-protection tunnel in auctions. WDO has a tick of 0.5 point = R$5 (US$10k). Both have small retail margins (about R$100–150) and B3 maximum fees of R$0.50 (WIN) and R$2.12 (WDO).

### Inferences
- Circuit-breaker levels for Ibovespa derivatives follow the spot index halts. These are commonly cited as -10%, -15% and -20%, but that was not verified here; check B3's procedures manual. WIN expires on even months (Wednesday nearest the 15th) and WDO monthly (first business day). That is standard market knowledge but was not sourced in this pass, so confirm it against the contract specs before encoding a roll calendar.
- Because hours shift with US daylight-saving time, rules keyed to clock time (for example "first 30 minutes", or the 10:30 US-open effect) must use the actual session schedule for each date. With 5 years of bars this needs a dated schedule table built from these circulars.
